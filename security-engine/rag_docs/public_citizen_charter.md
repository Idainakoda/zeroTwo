title: Citizen Charter - Revenue Department
classification: PUBLIC
---
The Revenue Department provides land record certificates, income certificates, caste certificates and domicile certificates to citizens.

Income certificates are issued within 7 working days of a complete application. Caste and domicile certificates are issued within 15 working days.

Applications can be submitted online on the e-District portal or at any Common Service Centre. The fee for each certificate is Rs 30.

Citizens can track application status using the application number sent by SMS. Grievances can be raised on the CM Helpline 1076 or through the grievance portal.

Office hours are 10:00 AM to 5:30 PM, Monday to Friday, except gazetted holidays.
