id: DOC-INT-01
title: Leave and Attendance Rules for Staff
classification: INTERNAL
---
Every employee is entitled to 8 days of casual leave and 30 days of earned leave per calendar year. Casual leave cannot be combined with earned leave.

Biometric attendance is mandatory for all staff from 1 November 2026. Arrival after 10:15 AM on more than three days in a month is treated as half a day of casual leave.

Medical leave of more than three days requires a certificate from a registered medical practitioner.

Leave applications must be submitted on the e-Office portal and approved by the reporting officer before the leave begins, except in emergencies.
