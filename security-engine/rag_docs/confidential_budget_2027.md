classification: CONFIDENTIAL
departments: Finance, Revenue

The draft budget for 2027-28 proposes a total outlay of Rs 4,860 crore for the department, an increase of 11 percent over the previous year.

Proposed allocations: land records digitisation Rs 620 crore, disaster relief fund Rs 1,150 crore, staff salaries Rs 1,940 crore, new tehsil buildings Rs 410 crore.

The proposal to merge 14 small tehsils into 6 larger units will save an estimated Rs 85 crore per year. This proposal has not been announced and must not be discussed outside the Finance Committee.

The draft will be placed before the Cabinet in January 2027.
