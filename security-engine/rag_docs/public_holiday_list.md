title: Gazetted Holiday List 2026
classification: PUBLIC
---
Gazetted holidays for 2026 include Republic Day (26 January), Holi (4 March), Eid ul-Fitr (21 March), Independence Day (15 August), Gandhi Jayanti (2 October), Dussehra (20 October) and Diwali (8 November).

Restricted holidays: each employee may choose any two restricted holidays from the published list, with prior approval of the head of office.

When a gazetted holiday falls on a Sunday, no substitute holiday is granted.
