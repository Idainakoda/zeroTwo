title: IT Helpdesk Standard Operating Procedure
classification: INTERNAL
---
Staff must raise IT issues through the helpdesk portal. Priority 1 issues (network down, e-Office unavailable) are resolved within 4 hours.

Passwords for e-Office must be changed every 90 days and must contain at least 12 characters. Passwords must never be shared over phone, email or chat, including with IT staff.

Laptops must have disk encryption enabled. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT cell within 2 hours.

Use of personal AI chatbots for official files is not permitted. Only the department's approved AI assistant, which runs through the security gateway, may be used.
